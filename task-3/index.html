What TechBridge is

TechBridge is a practical training initiative by Baselink Services Limited, built for students, graduates and career-switchers looking for a landing. Instead of another set of lectures, you work on real, structured projects with guidance from people who've done the job — and walk away with something to show for it.

Our current programs sit in two of the most in-demand technical skill areas today, and every program leads toward the same thing: a chance to apply what you've learned inside a real internship.

Our goal is to help you bridge the gap between learning and real-world experience, so you can confidently step into your next role. With us, there are no more imposters in the room. Just professionals ready to deliver.

The 30-Day Practical Internship

Every TechBridge track leads here. Over 30 days, you work on a real, project-based brief — not a tutorial — with feedback along the way, the same way you'd work inside an actual team.

  • Project-based work, not passive lessons — you build something real from a brief, the way a working developer or analyst would.
  • Guided structure, so you always know what you're working toward and why.
  • A recommendation letter is possible for interns who complete the program successfully, based on your participation and performance.
