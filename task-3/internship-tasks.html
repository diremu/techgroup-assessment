The Internship Journey

8 tasks. 30 days. One shipped platform.

The TechBridge internship is a structured, project-based path. Each task builds on the last — starting with the basics of the TechBridge website itself, and ending with a complete internship platform.

How the 30 days are structured

Instead of one big brief, the internship is broken into 8 practical tasks spread across 30 days. Every task ships something real, and the tasks get progressively more challenging as you move from a static website to an interactive, full platform.

  • 8 tasks, spaced roughly every 3–4 days.
  • Each task builds directly on the work before it.
  • Difficulty moves from beginner fundamentals to intermediate, full-platform work.
