The Internship Journey

Two tracks. 30 days. Pick yours.

TechBridge runs two internship tracks — Data Analytics and Web Development. Both follow the same 8-task, 30-day structure. Switch between them below to see exactly what each one involves.

How the 30 days are structured

Instead of one big brief, each track is broken into 8 practical tasks spread across the same 30 days. Every task ships something real, and both tracks get progressively more challenging — from beginner fundamentals to full, intermediate-level work.

  • 8 tasks per track, spaced roughly every 3–4 days.
  • Each task builds directly on the work before it.
  • Difficulty moves from beginner fundamentals to intermediate work, whichever track you choose.
